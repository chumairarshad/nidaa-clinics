CLINICAL TREATMENT & REHABILITATION PROGRAMS

Evidence-Based Psychiatric Care & Rehabilitation in Lahore

From medically supervised detoxification to residential inpatient rehabilitation, Psychosis/MDD/OCD care, and hands-on clinical psychotherapy under Medical Director Dr. Bilal Warraich (UNODC American Qualified).

PHASE 1 • INTAKE
🧪

Medically Supervised Drug Detoxification

Safe, physician-monitored withdrawal management for Crystal Meth (Ice), Heroin, Opioids, Sedatives, Benzodiazepines, and Alcohol.

  • 24/7 Vital Monitoring & Physician Oversight
  • Symptom-Targeted Pharmacological Taper
  • Nutritional Restoration & Rehydration
  • Comfortable, Private Medical Detox Suites
PHASE 2 • RESIDENTIAL
🏥

Inpatient Residential Rehabilitation

Structured 30, 60, and 90-day comprehensive residential recovery programs in our peaceful, state-of-the-art facility in Lahore.

  • Structured Daily Wellness Routine
  • Individual Daily Therapy with Psychologists
  • Small-Cohort Psychoeducational Groups
  • Zero Access to Substances & Triggers
SPECIALIZED • PSYCHIATRY
🧠

Dual-Diagnosis Psychiatric Care

Simultaneous treatment for substance addiction and co-occurring psychiatric conditions like Depression, Bipolar, Severe Anxiety, and PTSD.

  • Board-Certified Psychiatric Evaluation
  • Evidence-Based Medication Management
  • Trauma-Informed Therapy Protocols
  • Integrated Mind-Body Recovery Model
MODALITY • PSYCHOTHERAPY
💡

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT & DBT)

Scientifically proven psychological interventions to identify destructive thought patterns, cravings, and emotional relapse triggers.

  • Craving Deconstruction & Coping Mastery
  • Emotional Regulation & Distress Tolerance
  • Relapse Warning Sign Identification
  • Behavioral Reconditioning & Goal Setting
FAMILY • RECONNECTION
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Family Therapy & Codependency Healing

Addiction is a family disease. We provide specialized counseling to heal broken trust, dismantle enabling, and restore family unity.

  • Codependency & Enabling Deconstruction
  • Healthy Communication & Boundary Setting
  • Family Psychoeducation Workshops
  • Post-Rehab Home Transition Planning
PHASE 3 • CONTINUING CARE
🛡️

12-Month Relapse Prevention Aftercare

Long-term recovery requires sustained support. Our 12-month aftercare system ensures alumni stay grounded, accountable, and drug-free.

  • Weekly Alumni Check-ins & Support Circles
  • Randomized Toxicological Screening
  • Vocational & Educational Reintegration
  • 24/7 Rapid Crisis Triage Assistance
STRUCTURED ROUTINE

A Typical Day at Our Inpatient Centre

Consistency, clinical structure, and calm mindfulness form the cornerstone of lasting recovery.

07:30 AM
Gentle Wake-up & Morning Vital Check Nursing staff check blood pressure, temperature, and administer morning medications in private suites.
08:30 AM
Nutritious Chef-Prepared Breakfast Organic, balanced nutrition tailored by clinical dietitians to repair physical damage from substance use.
09:30 AM
Morning Mindfulness & Light Physical Wellness Guided breathing exercises, stretching, and mental grounding on our peaceful clinic grounds.
11:00 AM
Group Psychotherapy & Psychoeducation Clinician-facilitated discussion on emotional resilience, overcoming stigma, and peer accountability.
01:00 PM
Healthy Lunch & Restorative Rest Period Nutritious dining followed by quiet relaxation and personal reflection time.

One-on-One Individual CBT Session Confidential 50-minute personal psychotherapy with assigned clinical psychologist.
04:30 PM

06:30 PM
Relapse Prevention Seminar Evidence-based strategies to identify early warning signs and create strong defense mechanisms.
08:00 PM
Dinner & Family Connection Hour Evening meal followed by scheduled, supervised phone or video calls with loved ones.

Quiet Reflection, Sleep Hygiene & Lights Out Relaxing herbal tea, reading, and restful sleep in safe, monitored inpatient quarters.
